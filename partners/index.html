How It Runs

Five steps

  1. Intro by DM or emailYou tell me what launches and when. I say yes only if it fits my audience, and send a quote.
  2. I run itI install and use your product on my own machine. No result, no post.
  3. Storyboard · day 2Stills of every beat for you to check before the film is built.
  4. Film and fact check · day 5You check facts and claims. The opinions stay mine.
  5. Post and reportPosted at a time we agree on, with real numbers at 48 h and 7 days.

What you can count on

  • Every paid post is clearly labelled as paid
  • Real UI and real output. Illustrations are labelled on screen
  • Numbers reported as X shows them, with dates
  • The clean master file for your own channels

What I don't do

  • Claim results I haven't tested myself
  • Fake reviews, testimonials or customer quotes
  • Pass off a sample as client work
  • Promise views, follows or virality
